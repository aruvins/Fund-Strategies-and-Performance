─────────────────────────────────

SIZE: ~1,500 lines
PURPOSE: Main analysis implementation

  1. Loads Fama-French factors (or generates synthetic data)

  3. Estimates rolling betas
  4. Constructs beta-sorted portfolios

KEY CONCEPTS TO UNDERSTAND
================================================================================

Beta (β):
  • Measure of systematic risk
  • High beta = high volatility relative to market
  • Low beta = low volatility relative to market
  • Beta > 1: More volatile than market
  • Beta < 1: Less volatile than market

Alpha (α):
  • Risk-adjusted excess return
  • Return above what CAPM predicts
  • Positive alpha = outperformance (good)
  • Negative alpha = underperformance (bad)

Sharpe Ratio:
  • Return per unit of risk (volatility)
  • Higher is better
  • Allows comparison across different portfolios
  • Typical value: 0.3-0.7 for stock portfolios

Market-Neutral:
  • Portfolio with zero market beta
  • Doesn't move with overall market
  • Returns independent of market direction
  • BAB factor is constructed to be market-neutral

CAPM Alpha:
  • Return explained by one risk factor (market return)
  • Equation: alpha = return - [rf + beta * (market return - rf)]

Fama-French 3-Factor Alpha:
  • Return after accounting for market, size, and value factors
  • More complete picture than CAPM
  • Often smaller than CAPM alpha (other factors explain some returns)
